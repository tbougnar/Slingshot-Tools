### What was changed

The original is a 54.9 second, 450 kbps Ogg Vorbis loop. The copy here is
trimmed to 40 seconds and encoded to 112 kbps MP3, with a 1.5 second fade in
and a 2 second fade out so the bed can be looped or cut short without a click.
No other processing was applied, and the music itself is unaltered.

### Why this track

It is instrumental, dark and ambient at a slow tempo, which is what the
narration needs: a bed that sits under a spoken voice without competing with
it. CC0 was chosen over the CC-BY tracks on the same site because these
Shorts promote a paid product, and only CC0 and CC BY are unambiguously safe
for commercial use with no strings attached.
